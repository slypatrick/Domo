# Domo — brand brief and logo process (Nov 2026)

## 1. Diagnosis: why the earlier rounds failed

**Process failure.** Rounds 1–9 started from the name's etymology ("domo" = home) and from visual
references, not from the business. The strategy package (`docs/strategy-2027/`) was never read.

**Critique of v9 ("two houses"), scorecard /5:**

| Dimension | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Idea | 2 | Three ideas stacked: houses + m + travelling dot + "person with arms". Needs a paragraph. |
| Simplicity | 2 | Two roofs, two doors, a step, a dot, a wonky base: five elements. |
| Distinction | 2 | Houses are the real-estate and home-services cliché. |
| Memorability | 3 | Colourful, but no single feature to recall. |
| Relevance | **1** | Says "home / real estate / delivery". Domo is none of these (03 §1: "not a marketplace, not a store builder, not a courier"). |
| Craft | 2 | Deliberate wonk reads as error; the step between roofs is awkward; the wordmark m clashes with round letters. |
| Versatility | 3 | Works small, but the icon needs a corner cut to look intentional. |
| Longevity | 3 | — |
| Colour | 3 | Palette grew to six colours with no rule. |
| Typography | 3 | Geometric wordmark fine; the house-m breaks its rhythm. |
| **Total** | **24/50** | Fatal flaw: wrong message. |

Repeated concepts across rounds: the door, the house, the coral dot as "light", tiles. Patterns were added before
a mark existed. A system must grow from one idea, not hold several.

## 2. What the business is (from the strategy package)

- **Category:** COD order confirmation and tracking for social sellers — a "bureau de commandes".
- **Mission:** help Tunisian online sellers ship only the orders that will be accepted.
- **Promise:** « Chaque commande confirmée avant d'être expédiée. » Headline: « Moins de colis refusés. »
- **How it works:** the seller sends a link; the buyer confirms items, price and address on their own phone in one
  tap; the seller ships only confirmed orders and sees what was delivered and why parcels came back.
- **Main competitor:** WhatsApp + notebook + phone calls. Others: store builders (Converty, YouCan, TikTak Pro),
  COD ops tools (eGrow), couriers' dashboards.
- **Claims rule:** say « confirmée par le client », never « vérifiée » or « garantie ».
- **Tone:** direct, practical, seller-to-seller. Numbers over adjectives.

## 3. Customers and where they meet the mark

| Who | Who they are | Where they see Domo | What the mark must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seller** (pays) | Instagram/Facebook seller of fashion, accessories, beauty; 50–400 orders/month; Grand Tunis first; works from a phone, often alone | App icon on the home screen (daily), dashboard, Reels/TikTok demos, seller groups | Look like a serious work tool, but native to social media; be proud to show it |
| **Buyer** (never signs up) | Anyone in Tunisia ordering from a page, pays cash at the door | **A link in WhatsApp from a seller**: preview image, favicon, the confirmation page | Look legitimate in two seconds, not like a scam link; calm and clear |
| Courier partner (later) | Delivery companies | Exported files, co-marketing | Neutral, professional |

The buyer moment is the brand's moment of truth: one tap that says « oui, je confirme ».

## 4. Design brief

**Summary:** Domo lets Tunisian social sellers ship only orders the buyer has confirmed — fewer refused parcels.
**Audience:** sellers first (daily app use), buyers second (one moment, via WhatsApp).
**Brand adjectives:** **sure, clear, quick, friendly, local.** **Avoid feeling:** bureaucratic, "bank-secure", cute/childish, courier/speed.
**Big idea:** *the confirmation* — the order that was open becomes certain.
**Category look to avoid:** delivery trucks, speed lines, parcels, shopping bags and carts, location pins, shields and padlocks, the generic check-in-a-circle "verified" badge, WhatsApp's double tick.
**Open territory:** an identity built on *the moment of confirmation* rather than on delivery or commerce.
**Mark type hypothesis:** a custom **wordmark** with one ownable detail, plus an app icon derived from it. Reason: a young product with a short, pleasant name; it must be learnt as a word sellers say and type; buyers read the name in a link.
**Must work on:** 16 px favicon in a WhatsApp/Chrome tab, Android/iOS icon, WhatsApp link preview, dark mode, one colour, Reels end card, Arabic layer later.
**Constraints:** keep Domo blue `#1D3FD1` (the app's theme) and coral `#FF5A3C` (the existing accent); two colours plus ink. No guarantee or verification symbols.
**Success criteria:** one sentence explains it; reads as « domo » in one glance; recognisable at 16 px; no house, truck, pin, shield, cart.
**Assumptions to confirm:** the name stays « Domo »; the primary language of the mark is Latin script, with an Arabic version later.

## 5. Word map (intersections in bold)

- confirm → yes, oui, « إي », tap, seal, signature, full stop, closed loop, click, handshake, agreement
- order → message, DM, link, form, list, parcel, address
- sure → full, complete, solid, closed, final, **point final**
- clear → simple, open/closed, before/after, status
- domo → d · o · m · o, two o's, round letters, « do »
- **the second o of domo closes: open → full = pending → confirmed (point final)**
- **the order starts as a DM: d + speech bubble**
- **two parties agree: two halves that meet and complete one shape**
