Domo — de maison à maison

Domo means home. In the logo, the m is two little houses sharing one wall: the home where it is made, and the home where it arrives. The shared wall is Domo, the bond of trust between them. The coral dot hops from roof to roof — the order on its way — and, at a glance, the mark becomes a person welcoming you with open arms.
