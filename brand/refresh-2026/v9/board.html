Domo — de maison à maison

Two different homes sharing one wall: the home where it is made and the home where it arrives. Domo is the wall between them — the trust. The coral dot is the order hopping from one roof to the other. Drawn bold and a little by hand, like everything homemade. Together the two houses still spell the m of domo.
